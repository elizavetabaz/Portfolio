What's actually broken, and how I fix it

Most companies don't need another idea. They need someone who can turn the idea into content, campaigns, and results, and stay with it long enough to prove it worked.

Scattered marketing efforts

Content, campaigns, and reporting live in different tools, run by different people, with no one connecting them. I step in as the single owner who ties strategy to execution, so nothing falls through the gap between departments.

Content that doesn't convert

Plenty of teams produce content. Fewer produce content that ranks, gets read, and drives the next click. I write and structure content around what the audience actually searches for and needs, not just what's easy to publish.

Leads that go nowhere

A full pipeline means nothing if leads stall after the first email. I build the HubSpot funnel, lead scoring, and nurture flows that move a contact from first touch to sales-ready, and report on what's actually working.

Brand voice that breaks across markets

A message that works in English doesn't always land the same way in Romanian or Russian. I adapt tone and strategy market by market without losing what makes the brand recognizable.

AI output nobody trusts

Teams either avoid AI entirely or ship its output unchecked. I build AI-powered workflows that speed up production, then review everything critically before it goes out, so speed never comes at the cost of quality.
