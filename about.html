Experience

My path into marketing didn't start with a title, it started with the belief that the right words move people to act, and that belief has driven every role since. Most recently, that's meant managing a portfolio of B2B and SaaS client accounts end to end: onboarding, strategy, execution, all of it, backed by AI-powered workflows that let me scale output without handing off quality control to a machine.

That conviction got sharpened at Endava, where I owned the full HubSpot funnel as Senior Demand Generation Specialist. Lead scoring, nurture workflows, segmented campaigns, and weekly reporting that leadership actually used to make calls, not just file away. A campaign isn't done until you can prove what it did, and that's where I learned to prove it.

I've also taught what I practice, because explaining something well means you actually understand it. I lectured on Marketing and E-commerce at Universitatea de Stat din Moldova, a course backed by Google.org and Moldova's Creative Industries Association, built for the next generation of multimedia producers stepping into a market I already knew inside out.

Before that, at FusionWorks, I built a strategy that grew online presence by 20% and audience engagement by 30%, proof that showing up consistently beats the occasional big swing. Alongside that role, I ran Moldova DevCon as Event Manager, the country's biggest tech conference, and learned firsthand that a brand gets built in a packed room just as much as in a campaign deck.

At Crunchyroll, I owned employer branding and communications for a global entertainment company: internal comms, external PR, CSR partnerships, and the events that pulled them all together. What I took away: a global brand only works if the local conversation lands. At ISD Moldova before that, I built the company's social strategy from scratch and ran employer branding from the ground up, because somebody had to prove the function earned its seat at the table.

The earliest years were pure craft, content work for Pentalog and a French ecommerce platform, plus a marketing specialist role that taught me CRM and lead qualification from the inside out. I was writing for people who weren't reading in my first language before I even realized that was a skill worth having. Everything I do now still runs on what I picked up back then.
